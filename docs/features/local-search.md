# Local insta-search

## What it is for

To find "where did we see that?" in a second, without opening a browser and losing focus.

## What the student can do

- **Jump to the search bar** under the slide with **Alt+K** (Ctrl+K also works), or click it.
- **Type at least two characters.** Results appear as you type and are grouped by kind:
  - **Slides:** text on the slides of the deck that is open now.
  - **Notes:** the student's notes from every deck, labelled with the deck and slide they belong to.
  - **Terms:** dictionary terms whose name or definition matches.
- **Narrow the scope** with the *Scope* button, which cycles through All, Slides, Notes and Dictionary.
- **Open a result:**
  - A slide jumps to that slide.
  - A note opens its deck at the right slide.
  - A term opens it in the Dictionary panel.
- Press **Enter** to open the first result, or **Esc** to close the search.

## What it is not

Search is entirely local. It looks only at the open deck's slides, the student's own notes and the dictionary, and never searches the web. A quick web lookup is a planned feature that is deliberately not part of this release; see [deferred features](deferred.md#in-app-google-search).
