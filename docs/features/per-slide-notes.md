# Per-slide notes

## What it is for

Notes are most useful next to the slide they are about. Each slide gets its own note, so revisiting slide 7 later brings back exactly what was written during slide 7.

## What the student can do

- **Type notes in the Notes panel.** The panel always shows the note for the slide currently on screen, and its header shows which slide that is.
- **Use light Markdown** for structure: `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `` `code` ``, `- ` bullet lists, numbered lists and `#` headings. The **B**, **I** and bullet buttons insert the formatting for them.
- **Preview** the formatted note, then switch back to editing.
- **Never press save.** Notes save automatically shortly after the student stops typing, and right away when they change slides. The panel shows *Editing…*, *Saving…* or *Auto-saved*.
- **Clear a note** by deleting its text. An empty note is the same as no note.

Notes are also covered by [local search](local-search.md), so a phrase written weeks ago can lead straight back to its slide.

## Not included yet

- Autocomplete or suggestions while typing (see [deferred features](deferred.md#note-autocomplete)).
- Rich media in notes (images, drawings, attachments).
