# Slide presenter

## What it is for

The lecture slides are the centre of the screen. Everything else (notes, dictionary, todos) is arranged around the slide the lecturer is currently on.

## What the student can do

- **View the lecture deck** as large, crisp pages that fit the available space.
- **Move between slides** with the arrows in the slide header or the keyboard: left/right arrows, Page Up/Down, Space, Home and End. Click the slide first so the keyboard goes to it.
- **Zoom** in and out, and scroll around a zoomed slide. One click returns to *fit to screen*.
- **Switch decks** from the deck title in the slide header. The menu groups decks by course, and a search field filters by title or course.
- **Import a PDF** from the same menu. Imported decks are stored on the device and stay available in later sessions.
- **Remove a deck** from the same menu. Notes for that deck go with it; imported PDF bytes are deleted from the device.
- **Pick up where they left off.** Each deck remembers its last slide, and the app reopens the last deck used.

## Out of scope for now

- **Drawing or annotating on slides.** The pen button is visible but disabled; see [deferred features](deferred.md#slide-annotations).
- Presenting to a projector, speaker notes, or slide formats other than PDF.
