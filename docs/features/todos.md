# Todos

## What it is for

During a lecture, students notice things to do later: an exercise to redo, a question to ask, a section to reread. The todo drawer captures these in a couple of seconds without leaving the slide, so they do not interrupt listening or get forgotten.

## What the student can do

- **Open the drawer** with the **TODOS** button in the bottom bar. Close it with the × or Esc.
- **Add a task** by typing and pressing Enter.
- **Tick tasks off.** Finished tasks move to the bottom with a strike-through.
- **Delete** a single task, or **Clear done** to remove every finished one at once.

## The badge

The number on the **TODOS** button is how many tasks are still **open**. Finished tasks are not counted. With no open tasks there is no badge.

## Not included

Todos are a simple list for after class. There are no due dates, reminders, notifications or sharing, and they are stored only on the device.
